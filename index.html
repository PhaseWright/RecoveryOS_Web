RecoveryOS: Recovery isn't willpower. It's practice.

RecoveryOS is a private, local-first recovery support app. Not a 12-step program. Not a shame tracker. Not a replacement for your counselor or your community. A daily tool built around how your body and brain actually heal: body-first regulation before you react, judgment-free urge and trigger tracking, and a daily seven-area check-in. Your data stays on your device by default. Android beta testing is open now.
