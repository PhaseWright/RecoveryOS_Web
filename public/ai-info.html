What RecoveryOS is

What is RecoveryOS?

RecoveryOS is a private, local-first recovery support app for Android. It is not a 12-step program and not a simple sobriety counter: it pairs a daily seven-area check-in with body-first regulation tools (breathing, grounding, craving/urge techniques) so you can get through a hard moment, not just count days.

Is RecoveryOS a 12-step program?

No. RecoveryOS respects 12-step recovery and can be used alongside it, but it is built on neuroscience-informed frameworks (Polyvagal Theory, memory reconsolidation, ACT, dopamine recalibration, and more) rather than the steps themselves. It uses the language of biology and systems, which some people find lands better than character-defect language.

How is RecoveryOS different from an AA app or a sobriety counter?

Most sobriety-counter apps track days clean and reset to zero on a slip. RecoveryOS is slip-aware: a slip is logged as recovery history and context, not a reset to zero, and the app's focus is real-time regulation tools for cravings and triggers, not just a streak number.

Who is RecoveryOS for?

Adults (18+) in or considering recovery from addiction who want a private, structured daily practice alongside (not instead of) therapy, medication, sponsors, or mutual-aid meetings. It's built for people who felt like something was missing from other recovery tools, because the founder felt that gap himself.

Core features

Does RecoveryOS have a sobriety tracker or clean-day counter?

Yes. The dashboard shows a clean-day counter, streaks, levels, and XP, but it is slip-aware: logging a slip or lapse is stored as context rather than resetting your progress to zero, a deliberate design choice to avoid all-or-nothing shame spirals.

Can I do daily check-ins or daily logging in RecoveryOS?

Yes. The core free feature is a daily log across seven areas: nervous system, dopamine management, craving log, identity and mind, honesty check-in, connection, and nutrition, plus an end-of-day reflection, built to help you notice patterns rather than grade yourself.

Does RecoveryOS have journaling?

Yes, free-form journaling with gratitude replay is included in the free tier. Pro adds voice memo journaling. All journal content is stored locally on your device by default; see the privacy policy for exactly when and how data can leave your device.

What happens if I slip? Does my streak reset?

No. A slip or lapse is stored as recovery history and daily context, and it does not reset your clean-day counter to zero. This is deliberate, so one hard day doesn't erase months of progress or trigger an all-or-nothing response.

Does RecoveryOS have tools for cravings or urges in the moment?

Yes. The free emergency toolkit includes the physiological sigh (guided breathing), 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a mismatch protocol, cognitive defusion, and HALT prompts. Pro adds a 15-minute Urge Surf timer and a memory-reconsolidation timer that appears when you log a craving.

Free vs Pro

Is RecoveryOS free?

Yes. The free (Basic) tier is a complete daily practice tool on its own: daily logging, the emergency toolkit, journaling, history, backup/restore, app lock, and gamification. Pro is an optional subscription or lifetime purchase that adds depth, not a paywall on core safety tools.

What's included in the free version?

Free includes the full seven-area daily log, the slip-aware clean-day dashboard with streaks and challenges, the complete emergency toolkit, journaling with gratitude replay, month/year history views, local backup and restore, app lock, and badges; no subscription required for any of it.

What does RecoveryOS Pro add?

Pro adds Spark (a daily insights reader), three structured workbooks, guided NSDR regulation audio, an Urge Surf timer, voice-memo journaling, medication/supplement reminders, a 7-day mood sparkline, weekly summary notifications, and a personalized PDF progress report you can share with a therapist or sponsor.

Is there a free trial for RecoveryOS Pro?

Yes, Pro subscriptions (monthly or yearly) include a 14-day trial through Google Play Billing. A one-time lifetime purchase is also available. Exact pricing is localized and shown in the Play Store, since it varies by region.

Trust & privacy

Is my data private in RecoveryOS?

Yes. Recovery logs, journal content, and settings are stored on your device by default (local-first), and there is no required Recovery OS account. Data leaves your device only when you take an action that implies sharing, like exporting a report or connecting optional Google Drive backup.

Does RecoveryOS store my journal in the cloud?

Not by default. Journal and log data lives in local device storage. You can optionally connect Google Drive for encrypted backup/sync using your own Google account and, if you choose, your own passphrase. PhaseWright Labs never receives or reads that backup file.

Who built RecoveryOS and why?

RecoveryOS was built by Michael du Preez, a developer in Cape Town, South Africa, who is himself in long-term recovery. He built the tools he wished he'd had (grounded in neuroscience and lived experience) after noticing gaps between what mutual-aid programs teach and what the underlying science explains. His full story is at recoveryos.org/story.html.

RecoveryOS is live on Android through Google Play's closed testing track while it completes Google's required tester-enrollment period ahead of a full public Play Store listing. You can join the beta at recoveryos.org today, and public launch follows once that requirement is met.
